# ShopWave Return & Refund Policy

- Refunds are available within 14 days of the purchase date.
- One refund per order. Orders already refunded cannot be refunded again.
- Cancelled orders are not charged and are not eligible for refunds.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
- Items damaged on arrival qualify for a full refund within the 14-day window with photo evidence not required for orders under $50.
- Outside the 14-day window, customers may be offered store credit at a human agent's discretion. The support agent cannot issue store credit.
